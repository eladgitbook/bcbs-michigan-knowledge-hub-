# Member Services Guide

Reference guide for the Member Services team covering call handling, system workflows, and service standards.

## Service Standards

| Channel | Target Response | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Phone (member-facing) | Answer within 30 seconds | M–F 8am–8pm ET; Sat 9am–3pm ET |
| Secure message (portal) | Reply within 2 business days | 24/7 submission |
| Chat (bcbsm.com) | Respond within 60 seconds | M–F 8am–6pm ET |
| Written correspondence | Respond within 10 calendar days | — |

## Call Handling Workflow

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Greeting & Verification
Greet the member professionally. Collect: member ID or date of birth + last 4 SSN. Verify identity in **Member 360** before accessing any account information.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Identify the Issue
Use active listening. Let the member finish before summarizing the issue back to them. Common categories: eligibility questions, benefits verification, claims status, provider search, billing.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Resolve or Route
- **Self-serve:** If the member can resolve it via the portal, walk them through it
- **Agent-resolved:** Document the interaction and take the appropriate action in Facets/Member 360
- **Escalation needed:** Transfer to the appropriate team (see escalation table below)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Close & Document
Confirm the member's issue is resolved. Document the interaction in **Member 360** with call type, actions taken, and any follow-up needed.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Escalation Guide

| Issue Type | Escalate To |
|---|---|
| Complex claims dispute | Claims Operations direct line x5200 |
| Grievance filing | Member Grievance team x5300 |
| HIPAA complaint | Compliance Officer — do not attempt to resolve independently |
| Abusive/threatening member | Supervisor immediately |
| Media inquiry | Corporate Communications — do not comment |
