# Regulatory Overview

BCBS Michigan operates under a complex regulatory environment spanning federal and state requirements. This page summarizes the key frameworks all employees should be aware of.

## Key Regulations

### HIPAA
The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act governs how we handle Protected Health Information (PHI).

- **Privacy Rule:** Limits how PHI can be used and disclosed
- **Security Rule:** Requires safeguards for electronic PHI (ePHI)
- **Breach Notification Rule:** Mandates notification of affected individuals within 60 days of a breach

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Every employee handles PHI.** Even talking about a member's coverage in a public space is a potential HIPAA violation. When in doubt, ask Compliance.
{% endhint %}

### ACA (Affordable Care Act)
- Prohibits pre-existing condition exclusions
- Requires coverage of essential health benefits
- Mandates MLR (Medical Loss Ratio) reporting to CMS

### Michigan Insurance Code
- Governs our license to operate in Michigan
- Requires prompt payment of clean claims (within 45 days)
- Mandates specific grievance and appeal rights for members

### CMS Medicare/Medicaid
For employees working with government programs:
- Star Ratings requirements
- Part D formulary compliance
- Medicaid managed care contract terms

## Annual Compliance Training

All employees must complete compliance training by **June 30** each year. Training is assigned in Workday under **Learning**. Completion is tracked and reported to the Board of Directors.

| Course | Audience | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| HIPAA Fundamentals | All employees | 30 min |
| Code of Business Conduct | All employees | 45 min |
| Medicare Compliance (FWA) | Government Programs staff | 60 min |
| Data Security Awareness | All employees | 30 min |
