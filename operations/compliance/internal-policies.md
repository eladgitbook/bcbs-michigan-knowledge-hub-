# Internal Policies

Key policies that govern day-to-day operations at BCBS Michigan. Full policy documents are available in SharePoint under **Compliance → Policy Library**.

## Employee Conduct

| Policy | Summary |
|---|---|
| Code of Business Conduct | Standards of ethical behavior for all employees |
| Conflict of Interest | Disclosure requirements for outside employment, investments |
| Anti-Harassment & Discrimination | Zero tolerance for harassment; reporting obligations |
| Social Media | Guidance on representing BCBSM online |
| Gifts & Entertainment | Limits on accepting/giving gifts ($25 per occurrence, $75 per year) |

## Data & Privacy

| Policy | Summary |
|---|---|
| HIPAA Privacy Policy | PHI access, use, and disclosure rules |
| Information Security Policy | Device use, passwords, data handling |
| Data Classification Policy | How to classify and handle data by sensitivity level |
| Acceptable Use Policy | Company device and network usage rules |
| Vendor Data Sharing | Requirements before sharing data with third parties |

## Financial & Operational

| Policy | Summary |
|---|---|
| Expense Reimbursement | Allowable expenses, approval thresholds, submission deadlines |
| Procurement Policy | Vendor selection and approval for purchases > $10k |
| Travel Policy | Booking requirements, per diem rates, approval workflow |
| Records Retention | How long to keep various document types; destruction procedures |
