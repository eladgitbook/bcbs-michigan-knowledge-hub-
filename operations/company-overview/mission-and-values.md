# Mission & Values

## Our Mission

Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan is a nonprofit mutual insurance company committed to providing affordable, quality health care coverage to the people of Michigan. We exist to improve the health of our members and the communities we serve.

## Our Commitment to Michigan

BCBS Michigan serves approximately **4.8 million members** across the state and has operated in Michigan since 1939. As the state's largest health insurer, we reinvest in Michigan communities through:

- Local hiring and economic investment
- Community health programs and grants
- Advocacy for affordable coverage expansion
- Partnerships with Michigan hospitals and health systems
