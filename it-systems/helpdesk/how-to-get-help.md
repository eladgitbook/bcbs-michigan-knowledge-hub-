# How to Get Help

The IT Helpdesk is your first point of contact for technology issues at BCBS Michigan.

## Contact Options

{% hint style="success" %}
**ServiceNow is the fastest path** for most requests — tickets are routed automatically and you get email updates on progress.
{% endhint %}

| Channel | When to use | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| **ServiceNow** (`bcbsm.service-now.com`) | Most requests — software, access, general issues | 24/7 submission; staffed M–F 7am–7pm ET |
| **Phone x4357** | Urgent issues blocking your work | M–F 7am–7pm ET |
| **Teams: IT Helpdesk** | Quick questions, low-urgency | M–F 8am–6pm ET |
| **Walk-in (Detroit HQ, Room 1A)** | Hardware issues, equipment pickup | M–F 8am–5pm ET |

## Ticket Priority Levels

| Priority | Examples | Target Response |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 – Critical** | System outage, security incident | 30 minutes |
| **P2 – High** | Can't access key business system | 2 hours |
| **P3 – Medium** | Software issue, access request | 1 business day |
| **P4 – Low** | General questions, how-to help | 3 business days |

## What to Include in a Ticket

A good ticket gets resolved faster. Include:
- **What happened** — exact error message if any
- **When it started** — first occurrence
- **What you've tried** — restarts, other troubleshooting
- **Impact** — is it blocking your work completely or partially?
- **Device info** — hostname (shown on sticker on laptop) and OS version
