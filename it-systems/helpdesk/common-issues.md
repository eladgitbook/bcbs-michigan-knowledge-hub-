# Common Issues & Self-Service Fixes

Resolve the most frequent IT issues yourself — no ticket needed.

## Password & Account

**Forgot your password?**
Go to `https://password.bcbsm.com` and click **Reset Password**. You'll need your employee ID and registered phone/email for verification.

**Account locked?**
Too many failed login attempts locks your account for 30 minutes. Wait and try again, or call x4357 for immediate unlock.

**MFA not working?**
1. Check that your phone's time is synced correctly (automatic time sync must be on)
2. Delete and re-add the BCBSM account in Microsoft Authenticator
3. If still stuck, call x4357

## Email & Teams

**Outlook not loading?**
1. Close and reopen Outlook
2. Check your internet/VPN connection
3. Try the web version at `outlook.office.com` to confirm it's not a local issue

**Teams calls dropping?**
1. Connect via wired ethernet if on WiFi
2. Connect to VPN if working remotely
3. Lower video quality: **Settings → Devices → Video → 360p**

## VPN

See the full [VPN Access guide](../network-security/vpn-access.md) for connection issues.

## Slow Computer

1. Restart — many slowness issues resolve with a fresh start
2. Check for pending Windows/macOS updates and install them
3. Close unused browser tabs and applications
4. If persistent after a restart, submit a ServiceNow ticket with your device hostname

{% hint style="info" %}
**Pro tip:** Schedule a restart at least once a week. Go to **Start → Power → Restart** (don't just "Shut down").
{% endhint %}
